# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, evidence, and engineering tradeoffs.
- Respect privacy and never share real network or customer data.
- Accept correction and take responsibility for mistakes.
- Help maintain a welcoming technical community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate disclosure of private information, and publication of confidential data are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through a private repository security advisory. Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this policy. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project.
